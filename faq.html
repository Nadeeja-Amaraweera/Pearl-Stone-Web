Application & Process

Start with confidence

How do I apply for a job through Pearl Stone?

Apply via our website, WhatsApp, email, or office. We review your profile and guide you through each step until you are ready to depart.

What is the application process?

We register your details, collect documents, match you with employers, support visa processing, and arrange pre-departure preparation.

How long does it take to get placed?

Placement normally takes 4 to 8 weeks once your documents are complete, although timing can vary by employer and role.

Do I need to have previous experience?

Experience helps for skilled roles, but many employers accept motivated candidates with basic training and strong communication skills.

What documents do I need to prepare?

Bring your passport, national ID, certificates, medical report, police clearance, and any employer-specific documents required for processing.

Eligibility & Requirements

Understand the basics

What are the age requirements?

Most employers prefer candidates aged 21 to 50, but exact ranges can vary by job and employer.

What health checks are required?

A standard medical exam is required, including fitness checks and disease screening before travel.

Do I need to speak Hebrew?

Hebrew is rarely required. Basic English is usually enough, and we provide support for workplace communication.

What is the minimum education level?

Many roles accept secondary school completion; skilled positions may require vocational or higher education.

Are there any criminal background restrictions?

A clean record is usually required, especially for caregiver and household placements.

Visa & Legal

Know your rights

What type of visa will I get?

You will receive a legal, employer-sponsored work visa tailored to your job contract.

How long is the visa valid?

Most visas are valid for 12 to 24 months and may be extended with continued employer sponsorship.

Can I extend my visa?

Yes, your visa can be extended if your employer renews your contract and continues sponsorship.

Is the work legal and secure?

Yes. Pearl Stone works only with verified employers and provides legal contracts and visa support.

What are my rights as a worker?

You are entitled to fair wages, safe conditions, and ongoing support while working in Israel.

Salary & Benefits

What to expect

What is the typical salary range?

Salaries vary by role, but many placements include competitive pay plus housing or allowances.

Are there bonus opportunities?

Some employers offer bonuses for performance, attendance, or overtime. We share details before you accept.

What benefits are included?

Benefits often include accommodation support, health coverage, meals, and ongoing in-country assistance.

How are salaries paid?

Salaries are typically paid monthly through bank transfer or employer-arranged payment methods.

Are there hidden costs?

Pearl Stone is transparent about fees and explains expected costs before placement.

Support & Logistics

Care from day one

What support do you provide?

We support document preparation, visa coordination, travel planning, arrival assistance, and ongoing follow-up in Israel.

Will you arrange accommodation?

Many employers provide or help arrange accommodation. We also advise on safe housing options.

Do you help with transportation?

Yes. We coordinate arrival transfers and provide local travel guidance in Israel.

Is there 24/7 support available?

Our team is available for urgent questions and ongoing support while you are placed in Israel.

What happens after I arrive?

We help with final paperwork, employer introduction, accommodation setup, and settlement support.

Career & Development

Grow your future

Can I switch jobs after initial placement?

Switching jobs is possible if your visa and employer agreement allow it. We can advise you on the correct process.

Are there opportunities for promotion?

Many employers reward strong performance with promotion and better roles over time.

How long can I stay in Israel?

Your stay depends on your contract and visa. Renewals are typically possible with continued sponsorship.

Can I bring family members?

Family entry depends on visa type and employer approval. We explain your options once the placement is confirmed.

What about contract duration?

Contracts typically last 12 to 24 months, and the exact term is provided before you accept any offer.
